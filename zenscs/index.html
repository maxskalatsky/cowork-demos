Our Mission & Vision

Empowering seniors and individuals with disabilities to live with dignity, confidence, and independence through fierce advocacy and compassionate support.

Zen Senior Care Services is a proud 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

How We Serve Our Community

Medical Advocacy & Healthcare

  • Coordinate communication between patients, families, and healthcare providers.
  • Explain diagnoses, treatment options, insurance benefits, and care plans.
  • Support hospital discharges, care transitions, and follow-up services.

Housing Stability & Advocacy

  • Connect individuals with safe, accessible, and affordable housing solutions.
  • Protect housing rights and advocate against evictions or displacement.
  • Navigate assisted living placements and long-term care options with confidence.

Family & Caregiver Support

  • Educate families on Medicare, Medicaid, estate planning, and available benefits.
  • Provide one-on-one guidance through complex healthcare and financial decisions.
  • Offer peer support groups and resources to prevent caregiver burnout.

Community & Well-being

  • Foster meaningful social engagement to combat isolation and loneliness.
  • Connect seniors with volunteer companions, events, and transportation services.
  • Link individuals with community resources, referrals, and financial assistance.

Meet Our Leadership Team

Nara Murillo
President

Nara Murillo is the President of Zen Senior Care Services, a nurse, and a dedicated senior advocate with extensive experience in case management, healthcare navigation, and care transitions. She is passionate about helping older adults and their families navigate complex healthcare systems, understand their healthcare benefits, and access the resources they need to live safely and independently.

With expertise in Medicare, Medicaid, managed care, and senior housing, Nara is committed to improving quality of life through advocacy, education, and compassionate support. Her mission is to bridge the gaps between healthcare and housing, empowering seniors to age with dignity, confidence, and the care they deserve.

Barbara Slingluff
Chief Operating Officer

Barbara Slingluff is the Chief Operating Officer of Zen Senior Care Services and a registered nurse with extensive experience in case management, care transitions, utilization management, and senior advocacy. She specializes in helping older adults and their families navigate complex healthcare systems, ensuring they receive the right care, services, and resources at the right time.

With expertise in Medicare, Medicaid, durable medical equipment (DME), and post-hospital care planning, Barbara is dedicated to improving patient safety, reducing avoidable hospital readmissions, and supporting successful transitions from hospital to home. Her clinical background in cardiac telemetry and emergency medicine provides her with a strong understanding of the medical challenges seniors face and the importance of coordinated, patient-centered care.

Barbara is passionate about empowering seniors and their families through education, advocacy, and personalized support. Her mission is to help older adults maintain their independence, improve their quality of life, and confidently navigate every stage of their healthcare journey with dignity and compassion.

Jenny Probert
Secretary

Jenny Probert is a healthcare technology professional with more than 30 years of experience improving healthcare systems that support better patient care and care coordination. She is passionate about using technology to make healthcare more accessible, connected, and easier for seniors and their families to navigate.

Jenny is committed to advancing solutions that improve access to care, strengthen communication among healthcare providers, and support older adults in living healthier, safer, and more independent lives. Through collaboration and innovation, she helps further the mission of Zen Senior Care Services to enhance the well-being and quality of life of the senior community.
